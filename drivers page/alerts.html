Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do after an accident?
1. Ensure safety - move to safe location if possible
2. Call emergency services if injuries present
3. Document the scene with photos
4. Exchange information with other parties
5. Report the accident through the Accidents page
6. Notify your fleet manager immediately
How do I request roadside assistance?
Call our 24/7 roadside assistance at 1-888-ROAD-HELP. Have your truck ID and location ready. Services include towing, tire changes, battery jump-starts, fuel delivery, and lockout assistance.
Who do I contact for maintenance issues?
Report maintenance issues through your fleet manager at ext. 142 or email maintenance@fuelguard.com. For urgent mechanical issues, call roadside assistance immediately.
How do I report a safety concern?
You can report safety concerns anonymously through the Safety & Compliance email: safety@fuelguard.com, or call the safety hotline at 1-866-SAFE-LINE.
What's the process for fuel reimbursement?
Keep all fuel receipts. Submit expense reports through the Refueling page in your dashboard. Reimbursements are processed within 5-7 business days.
