Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do immediately after an accident?
1. Ensure safety - Move to a safe location if possible, turn on hazard lights
2. Call 911 - If there are injuries or major damage
3. Document the scene - Take photos of all vehicles, damage, and license plates
4. Exchange information - Get driver details, insurance, and witness contacts
5. Report in app - Use the Accidents page to file a detailed report
6. Notify dispatch - Call dispatch to inform them of the situation
How do I request roadside assistance?
Call our 24/7 roadside assistance at 1-888-ROAD-HELP. Have your truck ID, current location, and issue description ready. Services include:
• Towing to nearest service center
• Tire change for flats/blowouts
• Battery jump-start
• Emergency fuel delivery
• Lockout assistance
• Minor mechanical repairs
How do I report a maintenance issue?
Report maintenance issues through:
• My Trucks page - Click "Report Issue" on your truck card
• Call Dispatch at 1-800-DISPATCH
• Email maintenance@fuelguard.com

For urgent mechanical issues that affect safety, call roadside assistance immediately.
What's the process for fuel reimbursement?
Step 1: Keep all fuel receipts
Step 2: Go to Refueling page in your dashboard
Step 3: Enter trip details and upload receipt photos
Step 4: Submit for approval
Step 5: Reimbursements processed within 5-7 business days

Questions? Contact payroll at payroll@fuelguard.com
How do I access my pay stubs and tax documents?
Log into the Driver Portal and navigate to Payroll section. There you can:
• View current and past pay stubs
• Download tax documents (W-2/1099)
• Update direct deposit information
• Request payroll advances

For payroll questions, call extension 155 or email payroll@fuelguard.com
What benefits am I eligible for?
Full-time drivers are eligible for:
• Health, Dental, and Vision insurance
• 401(k) retirement plan with company match
• Paid time off and sick leave
• Bonuses for safety and performance
• Tuition reimbursement for CDL training

Contact HR at extension 188 or email hr@fuelguard.com
